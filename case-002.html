MISSING PERSON

Samira Vale

ARCHIVE VOLUNTEER · PHOTOGRAPHER

Samira was cataloguing old prints at Marrow House, a cinema that closed before sunrise. The projection booth was sealed at 1:48 a.m. No window was open. No one saw her leave.

At 2:13, her camera uploaded a photograph of a girl standing in the empty cinema hall. Police called it a delayed file. The time on the print says otherwise.

CASE 002 · SOLVED

The room was empty
because Samira left.

She took the spare key, left before the booth was sealed, and queued the photo for later. The “girl” was an old print from the archive. The disappearance was a trail she chose to leave.

On the back of the duplicate: “Where the harbor bell rings twice, ask for the first voice.”
